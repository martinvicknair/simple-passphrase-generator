About This Passphrase Generator

This browser-based passphrase generator builds secure but easy-to-remember login phrases using a simple formula: color + object + integer. It’s designed for customer support representatives handling temporary account resets where the phrase must be readable and easy to confirm by phone. Runs 100% in your browser; no data is sent.
